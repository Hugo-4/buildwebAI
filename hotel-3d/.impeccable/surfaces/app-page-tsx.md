# Surface: L'Arolle — home (one-page scroll story)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: international couples 30-55 comparing Courchevel addresses. Action: booking request (dates, guests). Proof: the place itself, 24 suites, 1850 m; no invented prices, awards or reviews. Constraints: 60 fps, Lighthouse > 85, lighter mobile, reduced-motion, AA, keyboard. Build path: code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A 1930s Swiss ski poster set in motion: radical scale jumps and oblique slopes carry a stay from arrival to departure. It refuses the category's black-and-gold drone-video hero with a centred white serif.

OWN-WORLD: Cold snow ground #F2F4F3, altitude blue #16245A as full-region fields, granite #121417 for ink, pale sky #BFD2E3, one warm note: arolle amber #C8873A. Bodoni Moda at poster scale set on a 12° slope, Jost 300 small caps for everything functional. Flat colour fields cut on diagonals, photo grain, no rounded cards, no gradients beyond atmospheric snow light.

STORY: The visitor arrives on the slope, sees the house as a speck on the ridge, descends into it, meets the suites, the table and the spa, picks experiences, and leaves a booking request; the night slope closes the loop.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL two-tone snow slope cutting the frame on a diagonal from lower left to upper right; blue sky field above. "L'Arolle" in Bodoni at ~22vw set along the slope, letters arriving one by one. A tiny chalet light on the ridge. Top bar: monogram left, "Menu" and the primary "Réserver" (dates, guests) right, always visible. Bottom-left: "Courchevel 1850 · 24 suites" in Jost caps.

FORM: Swiss ski-poster photomontage (Herbert Matter lineage), candidate 3 of 7 on my ordered list; seed:0c0a93f306cef259. Raises: key figures as fixed-position digit counters whose change is a visible mechanical roll (from nixie counter); clip-path reveals stay exposed and layer like collage (from décollage).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Signature interaction and motion grammar
Camera descends the slope with scroll; poster cuts slide in on the 12° axis; one easing family (expo-out for entrances, inOut for scrubbed travel).
